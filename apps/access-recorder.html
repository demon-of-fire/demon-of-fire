Access Recorder

The ultimate accessible audio recording studio for Windows. Record audio, import studio beats, monitor live input, and get instant local Whisper AI transcription — all with full NVDA screen reader support and global hotkeys.

Windows 10 / 11 Electron · Python Local Whisper AI NVDA Compatible Open Source · MIT

Overview

Access Recorder is an audio studio built around one idea: every feature should be reachable by keyboard and announced by your screen reader. It combines a full-featured audio recorder with on-device transcription so your recordings become searchable text without ever leaving your PC.

Key Features

  • Basic & Studio Pro Modes: Switch between a simple record/play workflow and a full studio with tracks and custom beats.
  • Local Whisper AI Transcription: Transcribes recordings entirely on-device — no cloud, no uploads, no privacy trade-offs.
  • Custom Beats: Import and layer your own backing beats underneath recordings.
  • Live Monitoring: Hear your input in real time while recording.
  • High Contrast Themes: Dark, light, and high-contrast visual themes for comfortable use.
  • Global Hotkeys: Record, pause, stop, and transcribe from anywhere.
  • NVDA Screen Reader Support: Every control is announced — menus, meters, and status messages.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + Shift + R — Start/stop recording from anywhere
  • Ctrl + Shift + P — Pause/resume recording
  • Ctrl + T — Transcribe the current recording
  • Ctrl + O — Open a recording
