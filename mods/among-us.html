Among Us Accessibility Mod

Among Us · BepInEx · C#

Makes Among Us genuinely playable by ear for blind and visually impaired players — no auto-completion, no hand-holding. Navigation keys, panned and pitched audio cues, spoken state read-outs, and manual confirmation, so gameplay and the alibi value of visual tasks stay intact.

BepInEx Plugin Playable by Ear NVDA / Tolk Speech Fair Play

About

A BepInEx plugin for Among Us that announces everything you need to play: your status, your tasks, the sabotage map, and where players, vents, and objects are — using spatial audio cues with panning and pitch so the game is fair and the experience is preserved instead of auto-played for you.

Features

  • Status read-out (H) — your colour, role, alive/dead, room, tasks done, players alive, and kill cooldown
  • Sabotage map (B) — open/close and trigger sabotages by ear
  • Spatial audio cues with panning and pitch to locate players, vents, and objects
  • Spoken task state and task navigation
  • NVDA / screen reader announcements built in

Requirements

  • Among Us (Steam / Epic / Itch), latest version
  • BepInEx 6 (x64)

Installation

  1. Install BepInEx 6 into your Among Us folder — extract into the game directory, run the game once, then close it.
  2. Copy AmongUsAccessibilityMod.dll into BepInEx/plugins/.
  3. Launch the game. The mod announces itself and reads out its controls.

Controls

Key Action
HStatus read-out
BOpen / close the sabotage map

The mod speaks all of its outputs through Tolk / NVDA, so no extra screen reader setup is needed.
