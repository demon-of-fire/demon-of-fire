Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website or use any software published by Demon of Fire, and how that information is handled. The short version: this website collects nothing, and the software is designed to process your data on your own device.

This Website

This website does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No cookies are set. No personal data is collected through this website.

The web server may retain standard access logs (IP address, timestamp, page requested) for a limited period for security and operational purposes. These logs are not shared with third parties and are not used for profiling.

Downloads and GitHub

Downloads and source code are hosted on GitHub, a third-party service operated by GitHub, Inc. When you download a release or visit a repository, GitHub's own privacy statement applies to the data GitHub collects (such as your IP address and download activity).

Desktop Applications

Desktop applications published on this site are designed to process data locally on your device. Unless explicitly stated in an individual app's documentation, no data is transmitted to Demon of Fire — there is no telemetry, no analytics, and no account system.

There are no accounts. This website and these applications have no sign-ups, no logins, and no user profiles. Demon of Fire has no way to access, manage, or act on an account you don't have — and you will never be asked to create one or to grant access to an existing account.

Some apps offer optional AI features powered by Google Gemini. These features only work if you provide your own Google Gemini API key in the app's settings. When enabled, the content you ask the AI about is sent to Google's servers under your key, and Google's API terms and privacy policy apply.

Your API key is stored only in the app's settings on your own device. It is never transmitted to Demon of Fire, never uploaded anywhere, and never visible to Demon of Fire or anyone else. If you use paid AI features, all billing happens directly between you and Google through your own Google account — Demon of Fire has no access to your key, your usage, or your bills.

Apps that use a microphone (for example, the voice assistant) capture audio only while you are actively using the voice feature, and process it locally. Local settings, playlists, and snapshots are stored only on your device in the app's data folder.

Game Mods

Game mods published on this site run entirely on your device. The AI features in the FC 26 and Rocket League mods are fully on-device (Florence-2 and EasyOCR); no game footage or screen captures are uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

This website may link to external services such as GitHub, Google, and Discord. Those services have their own privacy policies which govern data collected when you visit them.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please open an issue on the relevant GitHub repository or join the Discord server.
