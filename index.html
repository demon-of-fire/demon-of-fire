Built for Everyone

Accessible Studio is an NVDA-accessible video editor, photo editor, and file converter. It's fully keyboard-navigable, screen-reader friendly, and can be entirely controlled via natural language commands. Simply plug in a free Google Gemini API key to unlock context-aware editing and real-time project control.

Note: You'll need FFmpeg installed on your system to use the video and audio features.

The Toolkit

🎬 Video Editor

Timeline-based editing with multi-track support. Trim, split, add text overlays, and apply effects with ease.

🖼️ Photo Editor

Canvas-based editing. Crop, rotate, apply filters, and seamlessly remove or blur backgrounds.

🔄 File Converter

Instantly convert between major video, audio, image, and document formats.

🤖 AI Assistant

Tell the editor what to do. "Trim the first 5 seconds" or "Make it cinematic" actually works.

Just Tell It What To Do

The built-in assistant bar lets you type what you want to happen. Press Enter and it's done.

  • Video: "split at 10 seconds", "set speed to 2x", "remove audio from this clip"
  • Color & Filters: "make it cinematic", "boost saturation", "black and white"
  • Photos: "remove the person in the middle", "describe the image"
  • Smart Tools: "auto color correct", "close gaps", "export as mp4"
