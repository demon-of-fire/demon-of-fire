About Demon of Fire

I build software with a mission: technology and gaming should be universally accessible. Whether developing custom Windows tools, audio-driven game modifications, or AI productivity utilities, my focus is delivering rich, intuitive experiences where screen-reader compatibility and keyboard ergonomics are first-class citizens.
